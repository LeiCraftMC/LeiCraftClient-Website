Short answers to the questions players ask most, with links to the pages that explain more.

::accordion
:::accordion-item{label="Is LeiCraft Client free?" icon="i-lucide-circle-help"}
Yes. The mod is free, and so is every item in the shop. There is nothing to buy and no payments anywhere. Some items are **Exclusive**: you get those with a redeem code or as a gift, not with money. See [Exclusive items](/docs/using/exclusives).
:::

:::accordion-item{label="Is it safe?" icon="i-lucide-circle-help"}
- The mod is open source, so anyone can read its code.
- To link your Minecraft account, the mod uses Mojang's session check, the same one a server uses when you join. Your Minecraft access token never leaves the game, and you never type your Microsoft password.
- The website login only reads your Discord username, display name and avatar.
- Every community item is checked by a moderator before it goes public.

More in [Link your account](/docs/using/linking#is-this-safe).
:::

:::accordion-item{label="Which servers does it work on?" icon="i-lucide-circle-help"}
All of them, and in singleplayer too. LeiCraft Client only runs on your computer, so servers don't need to install anything. It only changes how players look — it gives no gameplay advantage.
:::

:::accordion-item{label="Do other players need the mod to see my cape?" icon="i-lucide-circle-help"}
Yes. Everyone who plays with LeiCraft Client sees the capes and cosmetics of everyone else who uses it. Players without the mod see your normal skin.
:::

:::accordion-item{label="Does it work with Lunar Client, LabyMod or other clients?" icon="i-lucide-circle-help"}
LeiCraft Client is a normal Fabric mod, not a separate client. It works in clients and launchers that let you add your own Fabric mods for Minecraft 26.3. Not every client can do that yet. The [compatibility overview](/docs/clients/overview) lists each client.
:::

:::accordion-item{label="Which Minecraft versions are supported?" icon="i-lucide-circle-help"}
The current release needs **Minecraft Java Edition 26.3** — exactly this version — with **Fabric Loader 0.19.5** or newer, **Fabric API** and **Fabric Language Kotlin**. See [Requirements](/docs/getting-started/requirements).
:::

:::accordion-item{label="Does it work on Bedrock Edition?" icon="i-lucide-circle-help"}
No. LeiCraft Client is a mod for **Minecraft Java Edition**. Bedrock Edition (phones, consoles, Windows "Minecraft for Windows") can't load it.
:::

:::accordion-item{label="Can I use it with an offline (cracked) account?" icon="i-lucide-circle-help"}
You can see everyone's capes and cosmetics. But you can't link the account, so you can't wear any yourself. That needs a real Microsoft account.
:::

:::accordion-item{label="Why do I log in with Discord?" icon="i-lucide-circle-help"}
Your LeiCraft Client account **is** your Discord account, so there is no extra password to remember. When you log in for the first time, you also join the LeiCraftMC Discord server. Your Minecraft account is linked separately, from inside the game. See [Link your account](/docs/using/linking).
:::

:::accordion-item{label="Can I wear different capes on different Minecraft accounts?" icon="i-lucide-circle-help"}
Yes. You can link up to **5 Minecraft accounts**. Each one has its own cape and up to 10 cosmetics. All of them share one wardrobe. See [Wardrobe](/docs/using/wardrobe).
:::

:::accordion-item{label="Can I hide other players' capes?" icon="i-lucide-circle-help"}
Yes. In the **LeiCraftClient** options, turn off **Custom capes** or **Custom cosmetics**. This only changes what you see. See [In-game features](/docs/using/in-game).
:::

:::accordion-item{label="Can I upload my own capes and cosmetics?" icon="i-lucide-circle-help"}
Yes. Create your community page in the **Creator Studio** and upload capes (PNG files) and cosmetics (made in Blockbench). A moderator reviews every item. After that, anyone can wear it for free. Start at [For creators](/docs/creators/overview).
:::

:::accordion-item{label="What are exclusive items?" icon="i-lucide-circle-help"}
Items from the official page or a partner page that you can't just save from the shop. You get them with a redeem code or as a direct gift. Once you have one, it stays in your wardrobe. See [Exclusive items](/docs/using/exclusives).
:::

:::accordion-item{label="Is LeiCraft Client open source?" icon="i-lucide-circle-help"}
Yes, under the **AGPL-3.0** license. The mod, the API server, the website and the skin viewer are on [git.leicraftmc.de](https://git.leicraftmc.de/LeiCraftMC/LeiCraftClient), with a mirror on [GitHub](https://github.com/LeiCraftMC).
:::
