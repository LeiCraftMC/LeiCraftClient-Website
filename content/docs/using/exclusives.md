---
title: Exclusive items
description: What exclusive capes and cosmetics are, how to redeem a code in your inventory, and how gifts from partners work.
---

Most items in the shop are free for everyone. **Exclusive** items are special: you get them with a redeem code or as a gift. There is nothing to buy — LeiCraft Client has no payments anywhere.

## What makes an item exclusive

- In the [shop](/shop), exclusive items carry the **Exclusive** badge. All other items say **Free**.
- Only the official LeiCraft Client page and partner pages can make exclusive items. Items on community pages are always free.
- On the page of an exclusive item you don't own yet, the shop says that you get it with a redeem code or as a gift, and shows a **Redeem a code** button.

Once you own an exclusive item, it is in your [wardrobe](/docs/using/wardrobe) for good. You equip it like any other item. On the website it's marked **Always yours**, in the game **Owned**.

## Redeem a code

Codes come from giveaways, partners and events.

::steps{level="3"}
### Open your inventory

Go to [Inventory](/dashboard/inventory) in the dashboard. If you're not logged in, log in with Discord first.

### Enter the code

Type the code into the **Redeem a code** box, for example `K7QF-2MXP-9TBA`. Upper or lower case doesn't matter, and spaces are ignored. Click **Redeem**.

### Done

The website says "Code redeemed!" and lists what you got. One code can give you several items at once. The items are now in your inventory and in your wardrobe. Equip them as described in [Wardrobe](/docs/using/wardrobe).
::

### Code links

A giveaway can also give you a link like `https://client.leicraftmc.de/dashboard/inventory?code=…`. It opens your inventory with the code already filled in. Check it, then click **Redeem**.

If you aren't logged in, the website asks you to log in first and then brings you back.

::note
On your very first login, the website shows a short welcome page first. After you finish or skip it, open the code link again, or type the code by hand.
::

### When a code doesn't work

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| "Invalid or expired code" | The code has a typo, has expired, has been used up, or was turned off. |
| "You have already redeemed this code" | Every code works once per account. |
| "Already in your inventory" | The code is fine, but you already own everything it gives. |
| "Too many redeem attempts. Try again later." | You can try 10 codes in 10 minutes. Wait a few minutes. |

## Gifts from partners

The official page and partner pages can also give an exclusive item to you directly, without a code. It shows up in your inventory and wardrobe on its own.

- You must have logged in to the website at least once. Before that, there is no account to give it to.
- The page finds you by your Discord account or by a Minecraft account you linked. Ask the partner what they need from you.

## Your inventory

[Inventory](/dashboard/inventory) lists every exclusive item you own, newest first. Each item shows where it came from: **Redeemed** (a code) or **Gift**, with the date.

If an item shows **Currently unavailable**, it can't be worn right now, for example because it was taken down. It stays in your inventory.

Free items never appear in the inventory. They don't need it — you save and equip them in the [wardrobe](/dashboard/wardrobe).

::tip
In the [wardrobe](/dashboard/wardrobe), turn on **Exclusives only** to see just the exclusive items you own.
::

## They stay yours

- You can't remove an exclusive item from your wardrobe. It has no trash button, only **Always yours**.
- Unlinking a Minecraft account doesn't take them away. They belong to your LeiCraft Client account, not to a Minecraft account.
- The page that gave you an item can take it back. Then it is also taken off all your Minecraft accounts.

::caution
If you delete your LeiCraft Client account (dashboard → **Account** → **Delete account**), your exclusive items are deleted with it. You can't get them back.
::
