The wardrobe is your own collection of capes and cosmetics. From it, you choose what each of your Minecraft accounts wears. You can use it on the website and in the game.

## What's in your wardrobe

Your wardrobe is not the whole shop. It holds two kinds of items:

- **Exclusive items you own.** You got them with a redeem code or as a gift. They are always there, and you can't remove them. See [Exclusive items](/docs/using/exclusives).
- **Free items you saved.** You saved them in the [shop](/shop), or you equipped them once (equipping saves an item, too).

You can save up to 500 items. If the wardrobe is full, remove something first.

## Wardrobe and loadout

- The **wardrobe** belongs to your LeiCraftClient account. All your linked Minecraft accounts share it.
- The **loadout** is what one Minecraft account wears: **1 cape** and **up to 10 cosmetics**. Every linked Minecraft account has its own loadout, so you can wear different things on different accounts.

To wear anything, you need a linked Minecraft account: [Link your account](/docs/using/linking). Without one, you can still save items.

## Save items from the shop

1. Open the [shop](/shop) and click an item.
2. Click **Save to wardrobe**. The box now says **In your wardrobe**.

You can also click **Equip on …** (with your Minecraft name). This puts the item on that account right away and saves it to your wardrobe. If you linked more than one account, pick the account in the **Minecraft account** list first. A cape replaces the cape you wear now. A cosmetic is added to the ones you wear, up to 10.

## Equip on the website

Open [Wardrobe](/dashboard/wardrobe) in the dashboard. On the left you see a 3D preview with your skin.

::steps{level="3"}
### Pick the Minecraft account

If you linked more than one account, choose it in the list at the top. With one account, the page shows "Wearing on" and its name.

### Choose a cape

Open the **Capes** tab and click a cape. To wear none, click **No cape**.

### Choose cosmetics

Open the **Cosmetics** tab. Click a cosmetic to put it on, click it again to take it off. Your equipped cosmetics are listed under the preview, for example **Cosmetics (3/10)**.

### Save

The preview shows every change at once, but nothing changes in the game until you click **Save**. **Reset** throws away the changes you haven't saved yet.
::

Above the items there is a search box, and the **Exclusives only** switch shows only the exclusive items you own. **Explore the shop** takes you to the shop to find more.

## Equip in the game

Open the in-game wardrobe in one of these ways:

- **LeiCraftClient** (title screen or pause menu) → **Wardrobe**
- the command `/leicraftclient wardrobe`

It needs a linked account. If the button is greyed out, it says "Link your LeiCraftClient account first". The wardrobe changes the loadout of the Minecraft account you are playing with right now.

- Switch between the **Capes** and **Cosmetics** tabs, and use the search box to filter.
- **Capes:** click a cape to choose it, or click **No cape**.
- **Cosmetics:** click one to put it on or take it off. The line at the bottom counts them ("Cosmetics equipped: 3 / 10").
- **Equipped** marks what you wear. **Owned** marks exclusive items you own.
- The preview on the left shows your selection. Drag it to turn your player and see the cape on the back. Your player in the world shows it, too.
- **Save** saves the loadout. **Cancel** or :kbd{value="Esc"} throws your changes away.
- **Explore the shop** opens the shop in your browser. When you come back, the list reloads, so items you just saved show up.

The in-game wardrobe also works on the title screen, before you join a world.

::note
You can't remove items from your wardrobe in the game. Do that on the website (see below).
::

## Variants

Some cosmetics come in more than one **variant**. A variant is made for one skin model: **Wide arms (Steve)**, **Slim arms (Alex)**, or **Any skin**.

By default, the variant is chosen automatically: the one made for your skin model, otherwise one for any skin, otherwise the first one. If you switch between a wide and a slim skin, the cosmetic follows. You can also pick a variant yourself:

- **On the website:** under the preview, next to an equipped cosmetic, open the list and choose **Automatic** or a variant. The list only appears when a cosmetic has more than one variant.
- **In the game:** click an equipped cosmetic in the list, then click the **Variant: Auto** button. Each click goes to the next variant, and after the last one back to **Auto**. The button is greyed out when the cosmetic has only one variant.

Don't forget to click **Save** afterwards.

## Remove items

Free items can be removed in two places:

- In the [wardrobe](/dashboard/wardrobe), click the trash button on the item.
- On the item's shop page, click **Remove** next to **In your wardrobe**.

This happens right away, without **Save**. If you wear the item, it is taken off **all** your linked Minecraft accounts. You can save it again from the shop at any time.

Exclusive items show **Always yours** instead and can't be removed.

## When others see your changes

- **You, in the game:** a loadout you save in the game shows at once. A change you save on the website reaches your game within about 10 minutes. To see it right away, click **Reload capes & cosmetics** in the **LeiCraftClient** options.
- **Other players with the mod:** they see your new look within a few minutes. The mod keeps each player's capes and cosmetics for about 10 minutes before it asks again.
- **Players without the mod** see your normal skin.

To check what you wear right now, open `https://client.leicraftmc.de/players/` followed by your Minecraft name.

::tip
A LeiCraft cape replaces your normal Minecraft cape for everyone with the mod. Like a normal cape, it also shows on your elytra. Choose **No cape** to show your normal Minecraft cape again.
::
